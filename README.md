# Drive-Folder-Sync-and-Backup
Backing up and syncing just specific folders to Google Drive (and being able to map what folders on Drive).

This was functionality that Google Drive Backup and Sync (desktop) used to have but took out for no reason.  It lets you backup and sync specific folders from your computer to Google Drive.  In addition, I took it a step further to let you map directly where in Google Drive it backs up to, instead of dumping everything in the root folder like the original app did.

It's also more barebones and minimalistic - no extra drive taking up space and bandwidth and also no slowing down Windows Explorer by attaching to the file context menus.
